A Trusted Name in Real Estate Advisory

Mehta Properties LLC is a Dubai-based real estate brokerage and advisory firm focused on connecting clients with property opportunities across the UAE and Indian markets.

We work with individual buyers, homeowners, investors and international clients, offering professional guidance throughout their property journey. Our approach combines market understanding, industry relationships and a clear focus on client requirements.

With our expanding presence in the Indian real estate market and associations with established developers, including Godrej Properties, we aim to create valuable opportunities while maintaining our independent advisory positioning.
